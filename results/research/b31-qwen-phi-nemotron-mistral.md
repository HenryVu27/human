Source audit for B31, read 2026-11-05. V means I read it myself in the named primary source. U means not verified.

Main answer: the recollection holds, and the reports say more than it claims. The Qwen reports describe instruction data in pretraining from the first 2023 report on. The Qwen3 report describes trillions of synthetic tokens in instruction and question-answer formats. For Qwen3.5 there is no data report at all. Independent evidence on Qwen base models (V, arXiv HTML):
- **Liu et al. 2503.20783 §2.2:** Qwen2.5 base models gain about 60% by dropping the template, and reach a 100% answering rate with no template. The authors "hypothesize that they may pretrain on concatenated question-answer texts". They call DeepSeek-V3-Base "a nearly pure base model".
- **Gandhi et al. 2503.01307:** "Qwen naturally exhibits these reasoning behaviors ... whereas Llama initially lacks them."
- **Shao et al. 2506.10947:** a "code reasoning" habit already present in Qwen2.5-Math explains why spurious rewards still raise its scores.
- **Wu et al. 2507.10532:** Qwen2.5 completes truncated MATH-500 problems; the authors blame contamination.
- **OctoThinker 2506.20512 §3.3:** adding QA and instruction data to Llama mid-training narrows the gap to Qwen.
- **Explanation:** the Qwen reports themselves document instruction and synthetic QA text in pretraining. The papers' causal claims are hypotheses, tested on math only, never on open prose.

RECOLLECTIONS THAT WERE WRONG OR OVERSTATED
1. "Qwen reports describe synthetic or instruction-like data in pretraining": confirmed, and it understates the reports. Instruction data is stated from 2023 on, and Qwen3 names QA and instruction formats at trillion scale.
2. Qwen3's generators were not only Qwen2.5-Math and Qwen2.5-Coder. General Qwen2.5 also synthesized data, and it rewrote the text Qwen2.5-VL extracted from PDFs.
3. The Qwen2.5 report states synthetic data in pretraining, but not instruction data.
4. Qwen3.5 is natively multimodal, but the 9B is dense, not mixture-of-experts. Ignore its "Post-training" label.
5. Design finding: Nemotron confounds H1 and H2, since it has both the newest crawl and the most synthetic data. Cleaner contrasts:
   - Phi-2: web corpora published by mid-2023 (snapshot dates U) plus heavy GPT-3.5 synthetic data.
   - Mistral-Small-24B-Base-2501: 2025 vintage, claims no synthetic data.
   - Falcon-H1-7B: an extreme case of rewritten data.
